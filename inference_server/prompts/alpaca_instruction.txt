Generate a BehaviorTree XML for the described robot task. Use explicit syntax: <Action ID="ActionName" .../> and <Condition ID="ConditionName" .../>. Output only valid XML.
When planner notes are present, emit exactly the listed Actions in their listed order unless their Structure requires nesting. Do not add actions. Copy every quoted entity_ref and task noun phrase verbatim; do not substitute examples, shorten labels, or invent blackboard pose values.
